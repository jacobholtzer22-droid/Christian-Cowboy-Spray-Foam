## What insulation removal is for

{config.displayName} takes out old insulation for homes and commercial properties across {config.primaryCity}, {config.secondaryCity} and {config.homeCounty} County. Old material comes out when it is wet, moldy, compressed flat, contaminated by rodents or pests, or simply in the way of what is going in next. It is removed before the new insulation goes in.

## Do I have to remove the old insulation first?

Not always. Plenty of attics are fine to top up, and if yours is one of them we will say so. The free inspection is where that gets decided, by looking at the condition of what is actually up there.

## Can you remove and reinstall in one job?

Yes. Removal and installation are both ours, so the old material comes out and the new foam, blown-in or batts go in without handing the job over to someone else halfway through.

## Why does it matter before spray foam?

Foam needs to bond to the surface it is sprayed against. Clearing the cavity gives it a clean surface to work with, which is why removal is often the first step on an older attic that is getting foam.
