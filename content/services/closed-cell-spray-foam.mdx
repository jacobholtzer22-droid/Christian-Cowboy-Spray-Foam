## What closed cell spray foam does

{config.displayName} sprays closed cell foam on metal buildings, crawlspaces, rim joists, basement walls and exterior walls for homes and commercial properties around {config.primaryCity}, {config.secondaryCity} and {config.homeCounty} County. It is the dense, rigid foam: it resists water, acts as a vapor retarder, and adds stiffness to whatever it is sprayed on. It carries a lifetime warranty.

## Why pick closed cell over open cell?

Three reasons usually. It handles moisture, which matters in a crawlspace or against steel. It is rigid, so it strengthens the assembly. And at about R-6 to R-7 per inch it gives roughly twice the insulation of open cell in the same depth, which decides it whenever the cavity is shallow.

## Does it work on a metal building or pole barn?

Metal buildings are one of the common applications. Foam bonds to the panel, seals the gaps around the framing, and the vapor retarder side of it deals with the condensation that steel produces on a cold morning.

## What about a crawlspace?

Crawlspaces, rim joists and basement walls are all standard closed cell work, for the same reason: they are damp spaces where keeping moisture out matters as much as the R-value.

## Is there a warranty?

Open cell and closed cell spray foam both carry a lifetime warranty. It does not extend to blown-in, batts or removal. Ask for the details in writing when you get your estimate.
