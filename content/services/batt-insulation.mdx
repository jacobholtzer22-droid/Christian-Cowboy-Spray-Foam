## What batt insulation does

{config.displayName} fits batt insulation between studs, joists and rafters for homes and commercial properties around {config.primaryCity}, {config.secondaryCity} and {config.homeCounty} County. Batts are pre-cut blankets of fiberglass or mineral wool, and they are the budget-friendly option when the walls are open and the cavity is a standard size.

## Batts or foam for a new build?

It comes down to budget and how the assembly is put together. Batts cost less and go in quickly while the framing is open. Foam costs more and seals air leaks as well as insulating. On a new build we will walk the job and give you both numbers.

## Faced or unfaced?

The facing on a batt acts as a vapor retarder, and which side it belongs on depends on the climate and the wall it is going into. That is not a decision to make from a catalogue, so we settle it at the inspection.

## Why do batts sometimes disappoint?

Because a batt only reaches its rated value when it fits tightly. Gaps around wiring and outlet boxes, or a batt squashed into a space too small for it, cost you performance. Cutting and fitting them properly is most of the work.
