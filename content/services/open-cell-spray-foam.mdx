## What open cell spray foam does

It is the lighter of the two foams: it expands a long way, fills the cavity, and seals the air leaks as it insulates. Open cell and closed cell spray foam both carry a lifetime warranty.

## How much insulation does it actually give?

Open cell foam is typically about R-3.5 to R-3.8 per inch. The number on its own understates it, because a large part of what foam does for comfort is stopping air moving through gaps that a batt simply sits next to. A sealed cavity at a given R-value performs differently from a leaky one.

## Will it make the house quieter?

Yes, and that is one of the usual reasons to pick it. Open cell foam is soft and absorbs sound, so it is often used in interior walls and ceilings between rooms as well as on the roof deck.

## Is open cell the right choice for my attic?

Sometimes, and sometimes closed cell is. It depends on the space, the depth available and whether moisture is a factor. We settle that during the free inspection rather than over the phone.

## Where it usually goes

Attics, on the roof deck and between the rafters, interior walls, and ceilings. If depth is tight or the space is damp, closed cell is usually the better answer; see [closed cell spray foam](/services/closed-cell-spray-foam).
