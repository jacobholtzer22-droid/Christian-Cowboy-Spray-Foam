{area.name} is a large city in {area.county} County, north of {config.secondaryCity}, and most of it was built out through the last two decades of the twentieth century and the start of this one. That gives it a very deep stock of suburban homes now old enough that their original attic insulation is well past its best.

## What we see here

Settled, flattened attic insulation is the common finding, and topping it up with blown-in is often the economical fix. Where the attic is being used as part of the conditioned envelope, or ductwork runs through it, foam on the roof deck tends to be the better answer.
