## Insulation in {area.name}

{area.name} sits between {config.primaryCity} and {config.secondaryCity} in {area.county} County, which makes it one of the easiest places for us to reach from either direction. Much of its housing went up in the second half of the twentieth century, so a large share of the attics here were insulated to a standard well below what is normal now.

## What we see here

Homes of that age are the classic case for either topping up the attic with blown-in or taking the old material out and starting again with foam. Which of the two makes sense depends on the condition of what is up there, and that is a look rather than a guess.
