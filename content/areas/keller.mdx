## Insulation in {area.name}

{area.name} is in northeast {area.county} County, a largely residential suburb whose housing is newer than much of the Metroplex and sits on comparatively generous lots. Larger floor plans mean larger attics, and a larger attic is where getting the insulation right has the most to do with what the house costs to cool.

## What we see here

Newer does not automatically mean well insulated. Plenty of homes here were built to the code of their day with batts and vents, and the owners come to us when an upstairs room will not hold temperature in August. Foam on the roof deck is a common answer.
