## What was done to them

- They arrived as iPhone screenshots, 1320x2868 with black letterbox bars top and bottom. The bars were measured per file and cropped off, which is why the heights are 2347 (9:16 frames) and 1760 (4:3 frames).
- Re-encoded to JPEG, which strips EXIF, GPS, IPTC and XMP. Verified empty afterwards. They arrived carrying EXIF.
- No upscaling and no filters.
- **IMG_7285 was cropped to remove a Johns Manville depth gauge.** The brand was legible at full resolution, and a competitor's material brand on the client's own site implies a product claim nobody has confirmed. The wide crop takes the attic floor below the gauge; the tall crop takes the left of frame, keeping the rafters.

## Resolution limit

Every one of these is 1320px wide, because they are phone screenshots rather
than camera originals. The image pipeline builds 640 and 1024 wide variants and
cannot build the 1920 it would normally use for a hero, so a full-bleed photo is
slightly soft on a large desktop screen. It is not noticeable on the texture
shots and would have been noticeable on a face, which is one reason the hero is
a ceiling rather than an installer.

**Ask the client for the camera originals rather than screenshots.** Sent
straight from the photo library instead of screenshotted, the same shots would
be roughly 3 to 4 times wider and every one of these frames would sharpen.
