## Compare Insulation Types (homepage component, three tabs) [general]

- Open Cell: expands to seal every gap, quiets the house, lighter and more flexible. Best for: attics, interior walls, ceilings, sound control between rooms.
- Closed Cell: dense and rigid, resists moisture, more insulation per inch. Best for: metal buildings, crawlspaces, rim joists, basement walls, exterior walls, anywhere depth is limited.
- Blown-In: loose-fill blown over and around attic framing. Best for: topping up existing attic insulation, filling irregular spaces, budget-conscious attic upgrades.

## Why spray foam (homepage benefits) [general unless marked]

- Seals and insulates in one pass: foam expands into the gaps batts leave open.
- Can lower heating and cooling bills by cutting air leakage.
- Closed cell adds a moisture barrier and stiffens walls and roof decks.
- Open cell quiets a house.
- Keeps conditioned air in and Texas attic heat out.
